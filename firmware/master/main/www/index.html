Passage calibration

—

Tunes this gate's thresholds on a real drone, its VTX on (this does not blind the gate). Step 1: hold the drone in the passage, where it flies through, for 5 s. Step 2: hold it 2 m away along the track, for 5 s. The thresholds proposed put a pass between what it reads at 2 m and in the passage. A pass may ring during step 1 (sound, a lap): reset that channel after.

    Detector (every gate)

    After a pass, the gate re-arms once the signal's 1 s average falls this many dB under its highest level since the pass; the next pass must then climb as far over the lowest level since.

    It lets laps count when a drone never goes far from the gate: a small indoor track, a strong VTX.

    Recommended: 16 dB.

    Laps missed on a small track or with a strong VTX: lower it, 12–14. Extra laps outdoors: raise it, 18–20. A lap counts when the gate pass stands about this much plus 2 dB above the far side of the track, where the lap reads lowest. 0 turns it off: the gate then re-arms only near the noise floor.

    The fleet's D is one value for every gate, kept by the master: sent now, then to a gate at pairing, when it comes back online and when it reboots. All gates sets that value and drops any gate's own D. A gate chosen alone keeps a D of its own the same way (own D 12) until All gates or Use the fleet's D. A gate whose C5 runs another than it should shows it next to its health (D 12).

    The C5's receive gain, forced (no AGC): 10 to 83, 40 by default. It sets how far a drone rises over the noise.

    Lower it when a strong signal clips (a VTX close to the antenna), raise it for a weak or far drone: the Passage calibration above says which.

    Recommended: 40.

    The C5 saves it and learns its rests again, so that gate is blind for a few seconds: every VTX off.

    Maintenance (admin)

    For a bench or a fault, not for a race: a radio calibration blinds the gate, and the debug console stays off until it is switched on.

    Radio calibration and reboot

    Every C5 calibrates its oscillators at each boot and the chip recalibrates itself while it runs: use these when a gate says cal needed. Every VTX must be OFF. Gates are blind for a few seconds.
