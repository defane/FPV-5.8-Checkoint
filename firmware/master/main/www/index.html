Tunes this gate's thresholds on a real drone, its VTX on (this does not blind the gate). Step 1: hold the drone in the passage, where it flies through, for 5 s.     Detector (every gate)

    After a pass, the gate re-arms once the signal's 1 s average falls this many dB under its highest level since the pass; the next pass must then climb as far over the lowest level since.

    It lets laps count when a drone never goes far from the gate: a small indoor track, a strong VTX.

    Recommended: 16 dB.

    Laps missed on a small track or with a strong VTX: lower it, 12–14. Extra laps outdoors: raise it, 18–20. A lap counts when the gate pass stands about this much plus 2 dB above the far side of the track, where the lap reads lowest. 0 turns it off: the gate then re-arms only near the noise floor.

    One value for every gate, kept by the master: sent now, then to a gate at pairing, on its save, when it comes back online and when it reboots. A gate whose C5 runs another shows it next to its health (D 12).

    Maintenance (admin)

    For a bench or a fault, not for a race: a radio calibration blinds the gate, and the debug console stays off until it is switched on.

    Radio calibration and reboot

    Every C5 calibrates its oscillators at each boot and the chip recalibrates itself while it runs: use these when a gate says cal needed. Every VTX must be OFF. Gates are blind for a few seconds.
