Detector (admin)

After a pass, the gate re-arms once the signal's 1 s average falls this many dB under its highest level since the pass; the next pass must then climb as far over the lowest level since.

It lets laps count when a drone never goes far from the gate: a small indoor track, a strong VTX.

Recommended: 16 dB.

Laps missed on a small track or with a strong VTX: lower it, 12–14. Extra laps outdoors: raise it, 18–20. A lap counts when the gate pass stands about this much plus 2 dB above the far side of the track, where the lap reads lowest. 0 turns it off: the gate then re-arms only near the noise floor.

The C5's receive gain, forced (no AGC): 10 to 83, 40 by default. It sets how far a drone rises over the noise.

Lower it when a strong signal clips (a VTX close to the antenna), raise it for a weak or far drone: the Passage calibration below says which.

Recommended: 40.

The C5 saves it and learns its rests again, so that gate is blind for a few seconds: every VTX off.

Passage calibration

—

Tunes this gate's thresholds on a real drone, its VTX on (this does not blind the gate). Step 1: hold the drone in the passage, where it flies through, for 5 s. Step 2: hold it 2 m away along the track, for 5 s. The thresholds proposed put a pass between what it reads at 2 m and in the passage. A pass may ring during step 1: it is a real one.

      Maintenance (admin)

      For a bench or a fault, not for a race: the radio calibration blinds the gate, and the debug console stays off until it is switched on.

      Radio calibration

      The C5 calibrates every oscillator at each boot and the chip recalibrates itself while it runs: use these when the health card says calibration needed. Every VTX must be OFF.
